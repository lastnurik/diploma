## Status and evidence rules

- **Draft:** a relevant local artifact exists, but its content or acceptance checks remain incomplete or unreviewed.
- **Pending:** the required action or evidence is not recorded locally; a placeholder or a proposed future action does not prove it happened.
- **Done:** every task-specific condition is evidenced, the student owner and different student reviewer have accepted the work, and applicable repository/review evidence exists. Actual project YouTrack setup is checked separately; the historical internal tasks do not each require a YouTrack issue. **No original task is verified done in this audit.** This does not mean that no drafting work has been done.
